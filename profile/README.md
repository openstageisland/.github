## 🎛️ For artists — take the stage

You do not need a track record, a follower count, a promoter or a booking fee. If you
make electronic music and want to hear how a live room reacts, the stage is yours:

1. [Join Second Life](https://join.secondlife.com/) (free) and log in.
2. Teleport to `secondlife://Derwent/248/128/22`.
3. Set up, hit play, and take the feedback — from DJs and producers who play here too.

**No fee. No booking form. No waiting for a slot.** You turn up, you play, and when
you are done the room empties and someone else walks on. That is the whole deal.

🎓 **New to DJing?** The island runs a **DJ school** — the fastest way to get good
enough to play a room. Ask in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/Z38YQ6Xmbp) and someone
will walk you through it.

## 🌃 For visitors

Come for one set, stay for the sunrise. Follow the lasers, find the bass, pull up a
chair, and say hello to someone you have not met yet.

There is nothing to buy, nothing to unlock and no list you have to be on. The stage
is the point. Turn up, stay as long as you like, leave when the sun comes up.

**🌈 Newcomers, LGBTQI+ residents and furries are explicitly welcome.** So is anyone
who has ever been nervous about walking into a room on their own. Especially those.
